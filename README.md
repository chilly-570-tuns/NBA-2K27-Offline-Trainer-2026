## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **NBA 2K27**. This trainer provides offline gameplay controls for NBA 2K27, focusing on stamina, takeover progression, scoring conditions, game clock behavior, and practice-session convenience. It is designed around NBA 2K27 systems such as ProPLAY animations, rhythm shooting, dynamic layups, the manual Dunk Meter, advanced dribbling, defensive contests, and MyNBA management.

**Target Audience:** PC players who want convenient offline practice, custom match testing, and adjustable gameplay conditions in NBA 2K27 without modifying online progression or competitive modes.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch NBA 2K27 and enter an offline match or practice activity before activating movement-related options. Unlimited Stamina is useful for testing sprinting, defensive slides, dribble chains, and repeated transitions. Disable the option before returning to menus or connecting to any online mode.

### Tools & Inventory

NBA 2K27 uses MyTEAM cards, MyPLAYER attributes, badges, boosts, gear, and VC-related progression. This trainer does not modify online inventories, virtual currency, card collections, purchases, or account data. Manage those systems through the game’s normal menus.

### World & Environment

Use the trainer in Play Now, MyNBA, and The W, which support offline play. Game Clock Control is intended for drills, custom scenarios, roster experiments, and franchise testing. Online areas such as The City, REC, Crew, and MyCAREER require connected services and should remain unmodified.

### Combat & Weapons

NBA 2K27 has no combat system; on-court contests are handled through shooting, finishing, dribbling, rebounding, steals, blocks, and defensive positioning. Finishing Boost, Shot Timing Assist, Dunk Meter Assist, and Defensive Stability are intended for offline testing of these basketball mechanics.

Designed for the current PC version of NBA 2K27 running through Steam. Use the trainer only with offline modes such as Play Now, MyNBA, and The W.
